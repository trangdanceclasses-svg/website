Patna Dance FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers about our dance classes, fee structure, batch timings, and studio located in Patna.

Which is the best dance class in Patna?

Trang Dance Class (Tarang Dance Studio) located at Ramkrishna Nagar, Patna is top-rated for its professional certified choreographers, spacious studio environment, and comprehensive courses in Urban Hip Hop, Indian Classical (Kathak & Bharatanatyam), Semi-Classical Fusion, Traditional Folk (Bhangra & Garba), and Creative Art & Craft.

Do you offer a free demo dance class in Patna?

Yes! We offer a 100% Free Trial Class for all new joining students. You can visit our studio in Ramkrishna Nagar Patna, try out a class session with our master trainers, and explore our facilities with zero obligations.

Where is Trang Dance Class located in Patna?

Our academy is conveniently located at Ramkrishna Nagar, NTPC Colony, Near Ram Krishna Police Station, Patna, Bihar 800027 (easily accessible from Kankarbagh, Bypass Road, and Rajendra Nagar).

What are the batch timings and age groups for dance classes?

We run multiple batches throughout the day from 7:00 AM to 8:30 PM (Monday to Saturday) and special weekend batches on Sundays. We have dedicated batches for kids (ages 4–12), teenagers, working adults, and senior practice sessions.

What dance and creative art styles are available?

We offer specialized training in Urban Hip Hop & Grooves, Indian Classical (Kathak & Bharatanatyam), Semi-Classical Fusion, Traditional Folk (Bhangra & Garba), Contemporary Flow, and Creative Art & Craft Studio.
